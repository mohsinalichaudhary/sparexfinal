RELIABLE PROCUREMENT SOLUTIONS

SpareX Technical Supplies is a trusted industrial supplier based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, delivering high-quality technical products and reliable procurement solutions across the region.

We specialize in supplying industrial tools, engineering materials, safety equipment, and specialized products that support the operational needs of defense, construction, manufacturing, maintenance, marine, and energy sectors.

With a commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction, we work closely with leading manufacturers and trusted suppliers to provide products that meet international standards. Our comprehensive product portfolio includes industrial tools and abrasives, pumps and hoses, lifting equipment, electrical tools and accessories, specialized raw materials, composite materials, HSE products, and a wide range of industrial consumables.

TAILORED SUPPLY SOLUTIONS

At Spare X Technical Supplies, we understand that every project demands dependable products, timely delivery, and professional service. Our experienced team is committed to providing responsive support, competitive pricing, and tailored supply solutions that help our customers operate efficiently and achieve their project objectives.

We believe that long-term relationships are built on trust, integrity, and consistent performance. By combining technical expertise with a customer-focused approach, Spare X Technical Supplies continues to be a reliable partner for businesses seeking quality industrial products and dependable supply solutions.
